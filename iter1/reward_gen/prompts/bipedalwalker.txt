# BipedalWalker-v3 reward design prompt

## Environment
Gymnasium task `BipedalWalker-v3`. A 2-leg humanoid must walk to the right
across a flat-ish terrain without falling.

**Observation** (Box, shape (24,)): hull angle, angular velocity, x/y
velocity, joint angles & velocities for hip/knee left/right, leg ground
contacts, 10 lidar rangefinders. Specifically:
- obs[0]  hull angle
- obs[1]  hull angular velocity
- obs[2]  x velocity
- obs[3]  y velocity
- obs[4]  hip1 angle
- obs[5]  hip1 angular velocity
- obs[6]  knee1 angle
- obs[7]  knee1 angular velocity
- obs[8]  leg1 ground contact (0 or 1)
- obs[9]  hip2 angle
- obs[10] hip2 angular velocity
- obs[11] knee2 angle
- obs[12] knee2 angular velocity
- obs[13] leg2 ground contact (0 or 1)
- obs[14:24] lidar 10-ray rangefinder

**Action** (Box, shape (4,)): torques for hip1, knee1, hip2, knee2, each in [-1, 1].

**Default reward** (for reference):
    +130 over an episode for reaching the right edge,
    -100 if the hull touches the ground (fall),
    +x_velocity per step (forward progress),
    -5 * |action| per step (control cost).

**Successful behavior**: forward progress without falling. Many viable gaits
exist (galloping, lurching, careful stepping).

## Your task
Design a reward function for PPO. There are multiple valid behavioral
strategies (different gaits). The reward should encourage forward locomotion
without overly constraining the gait.

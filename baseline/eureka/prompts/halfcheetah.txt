# HalfCheetah-v4 reward design prompt

## Environment
Gymnasium task `HalfCheetah-v4` (MuJoCo). A 2D planar cheetah with two legs
must learn to run forward as fast as possible. No falling / no early
termination — episodes are a fixed 1000 steps.

**Observation** (Box, shape (17,)):
- obs[0]   = z-position of front tip (rootz; height of torso)
- obs[1]   = rooty angle of front tip (pitch of torso)
- obs[2:8] = joint angles (bthigh, bshin, bfoot, fthigh, fshin, ffoot)
- obs[8]   = x-velocity of front tip (forward velocity of torso) — KEY signal
- obs[9]   = z-velocity of front tip
- obs[10]  = angular velocity of front tip
- obs[11:17] = joint angular velocities (same joint order as obs[2:8])

Note: x-position is excluded from the observation by default in HalfCheetah-v4.

**Action** (Box, shape (6,)): torque on each of the 6 joints, in [-1, 1].

**Default env reward** (for reference, you do NOT need to copy it):
    reward = forward_velocity_reward - 0.1 * sum(action^2)
where forward_velocity_reward = (x_position_after - x_position_before) / dt.

## Your task
Design a `compute_reward(obs, action, next_obs, done)` for PPO that
encourages the cheetah to run forward quickly while staying upright and
spending energy efficiently. You may shape the reward however you like —
forward velocity is in next_obs[8], pitch is in obs[1], joint velocities are
in obs[11:17]. Penalising large actions tends to help.
